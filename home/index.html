Cosmetology

A cosmetology course teaches students the art and science of beauty care, including hair styling, skin care, makeup application, and nail services. It provides both theoretical knowledge and practical skills to prepare individuals for careers in the beauty and wellness industry.

Nail Technician

A nail technician course focuses on the care and enhancement of nails, teaching skills such as manicures, pedicures, nail art, and artificial nail application. It combines practical training with knowledge of hygiene and safety standards, preparing students for certification and careers in the nail care industry.

Skincare

A skincare course teaches techniques for maintaining and improving skin health, including facials, exfoliation, and treatments for various skin conditions. It provides both theoretical knowledge and hands-on training to prepare students for careers in esthetics and professional certification.
