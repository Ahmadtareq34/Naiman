📘 User Manual

👣 How to Use the Naiman Website

  1. Create an Account: Click “Sign Up” in the top navigation bar and enter your details.
  2. Browse Categories: Through the home page or click the dropdown icon in the top left to explore all salon categories.
  3. View Recommended Salons: Head to the “Recommended” page to discover top-rated salons by category.
  4. Select a Salon: Choose a salon and explore its services, pricing, and contact details.
  5. Book an Appointment: Pick your services, select a time, and confirm your booking with payment.
  6. Manage Favorites: Click the heart icon (🤍) to save a salon. View and manage favorites from the top nav bar.
  7. Track Bookings: In your account menu, you can view all your current and past bookings.
  8. Explore Courses: Visit the “Courses” section to enroll in certified beauty & self-care programs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be logged in to book an appointment?

✅ Yes. You must be signed in to book services or save your favorite salons.

Can I view my previous bookings?

🗓️ Yes! Go to your account icon and select “Bookings” to view both current and past appointments.

What is the “Recommended” page for?

🌟 The recommended page shows the highest-rated salons across each category — handpicked for quality.

Is my payment information safe?

🔒 Absolutely. We do not store payment details — all transactions are securely processed.

Can I take a course without booking any services?

🎓 Yes! Our courses are available to everyone — even if you don’t use the salon services.

How do I find a specific category?

📂 Use the dropdown menu in the top left of the navigation bar to browse by category.
